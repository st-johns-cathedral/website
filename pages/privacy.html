Privacy policy

This site is designed to respect visitor privacy and avoid unnecessary tracking by default.

This website uses plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript without third-party trackers by default. Form submissions should be connected to a compliant endpoint such as Formspree, Google Forms or a church-hosted PHP handler. Any submitted data is used only for the stated purpose of prayer, contact, or news subscriptions and should be handled in line with applicable privacy and data protection requirements.

For updates, contact the church through the official contact page or other approved channels.
