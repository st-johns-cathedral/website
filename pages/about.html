About us

Mission, vision and faith

St. John's Cathedral, Nazareth is a worshipping and serving community rooted in Christ, the heritage of the Church of South India, and the pastoral mission of the Diocese of Thoothukudi–Nazareth.

Mission

To glorify God through worship, discipleship, prayer, service and the proclamation of the gospel in Nazareth and beyond.

Vision

To be a Christ-centred, spiritually vibrant, and compassionate church community shaped by grace, truth and service.

Denomination and Diocese

Denomination

Church of South India, formerly linked with the Church of England and the S.P.G. mission.

Diocese

Thoothukudi–Nazareth Diocese, where the cathedral serves as a place of worship, leadership and spiritual fellowship.

Statement of faith

We believe in the triune God, the Lord Jesus Christ as Saviour and Lord, the Holy Spirit as guide and comforter, the authority of Scripture, and the call to live out the gospel through worship, service, holiness and love.

Church beliefs

  • God is the Creator and sustainer of life.
  • Jesus Christ is the centre of our faith, worship and mission.
  • The Bible is the inspired Word of God and the foundation of teaching and practice.
  • The church is called to prayer, discipleship, unity and compassionate service.
  • The sacraments and worship are means of grace in the life of the church.
