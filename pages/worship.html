Sacraments

Baptism

Celebrated in accordance with biblical teaching and church practice.

Communion

Observed as a central act of remembrance, thanksgiving and fellowship.

Confirmation

For the public affirmation of faith and commitment in the Christian life.

Marriage

Holy matrimony in the context of Christian life and community support.

Funeral

Pastoral care and worship in times of loss, grief and remembrance.

Order of service

Prayer, hymnody, scripture, sermon, intercession and blessing in the cathedral tradition.
